What Should I Check Before Printing a Financial Report?

Before printing a financial report, take a moment to make sure that the information represents the period and financial activity you actually want to review. A printed report is a snapshot, so accuracy and context are important.

Check the date range first. A monthly report, quarterly report and annual report can tell very different stories. Make sure that the selected period includes all the transactions you expect and does not accidentally include information from a different period.

It is also useful to review account balances, categories and unusually large transactions before creating your final report. Small data-entry errors can become much more noticeable once financial information is summarized.

  • Confirm the report's date range.
  • Review important account balances.
  • Check for missing or duplicated transactions.
  • Review unusually large income or expense amounts.
  • Make sure categories are organized correctly.
  • Confirm that the report contains the information you actually need.

More information does not automatically mean a better report.
